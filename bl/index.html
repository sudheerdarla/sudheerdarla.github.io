Before your first trade

A little more
clarity.

What can I trade on BL?

Global equities, indices, commodities, digital assets and, soon, pre-IPO markets, all as perpetual contracts from one account.

Am I buying company shares?

No. Equity-linked markets give you trading exposure to a price, not ownership of shares, votes or dividends.

What is a perpetual contract?

A derivative that tracks the price of a market with no expiry date. Periodic funding payments keep the contract price close to the underlying.

How do stock-linked markets trade 24/7?

The contracts trade on Hyperliquid’s order books around the clock, even when the underlying exchange is closed, so prices can move outside market hours.

Are deposits, withdrawals and trades all in INR?

You deposit and withdraw in rupees from your Indian bank account, and your statement shows every deposit, trade, fee and withdrawal in INR.

How are wallets and execution handled?

BL connects your account to Hyperliquid for execution, so trades leave a public record you can check on the explorer.

What will trading cost?

Every fee is shown before you confirm a trade. Deeper liquidity can also mean less price impact, but actual costs vary.
