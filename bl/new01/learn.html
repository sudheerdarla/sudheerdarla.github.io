Glossary

Terms, defined once.

The words used across the site and the terminal, each with one meaning.

Perpetual
A futures contract with no expiry date.
Mark price
The fair price used for PnL and liquidation, drawn from several sources.
Funding rate
A periodic payment between longs and shorts that keeps the contract near the underlying price.
Initial margin
The collateral needed to open a position at a given leverage.
Maintenance margin
The minimum collateral to keep a position open.
Liquidation price
The mark price at which a position is closed because margin has run out.
Leverage
Position size divided by margin. 5× means ₹2,000 of margin controls ₹10,000 of exposure.
Reference valuation
The private-market valuation a pre-IPO perpetual tracks.
