About

Global markets,
settled in INR.

BharatLiquid is building one account that lets Indian traders reach global perpetual markets in rupees, with every cost and mechanic shown up front.

Why we exist

Indian traders know the mechanics.

Millions of people in India already trade F&O. They understand margin, leverage and expiry. Reaching global markets still means FX conversion, remittance paperwork and platforms that hide how fees and funding work.

We think that gap should close. BharatLiquid connects an INR account to Hyperliquid’s order books, so a trader in Pune can take a view on NVIDIA, gold or Bitcoin in rupees, on Indian banking rails, with timestamps in IST.
