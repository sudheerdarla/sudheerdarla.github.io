Common questions

Quick answers.

How long does an INR deposit take?

IMPS and UPI deposits usually arrive within minutes. NEFT follows bank batch timings. Timing depends on your bank. [Confirm figures.]

Can I withdraw to a different bank account?

No. Withdrawals go back to the bank account you deposited from, which must be in your name.

Why was my order rejected?

The ticket shows the reason, for example: Order rejected: insufficient margin. Required ₹18,240.00. Add funds or reduce the size.

What happens if my position is liquidated?

The position is closed at the mark price once margin falls below the maintenance level. Your statement shows the fill, fees and remaining balance.

How do I close my account?

Withdraw your balance, then contact support. We confirm by email.
