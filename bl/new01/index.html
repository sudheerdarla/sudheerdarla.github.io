FAQ

A little more
clarity.

What can I trade?

Perpetual futures on global equities, indices, commodities and digital assets, with pre-IPO markets planned. All from one INR account.

Am I buying company shares?

No. A perpetual gives you exposure to a price, not ownership of shares, votes or dividends.

What is a perpetual contract?

A futures contract with no expiry date. Funding payments between buyers and sellers keep its price close to the underlying market.

How do stock-linked markets trade 24/7?

The contracts trade on Hyperliquid’s order books around the clock, so prices can move while the underlying exchange is closed.

How do funding and liquidation work?

Funding is paid between long and short positions at set intervals. If your margin falls below the maintenance level, the position is liquidated. Both are shown on the order ticket before you trade.

Are deposits, withdrawals and trades all in INR?

Yes. You deposit and withdraw in rupees, and your statement shows every trade, fee and transfer in INR.

Is BharatLiquid registered in India?

Who can join?

Indian residents aged 18 or over who complete KYC.

How is execution handled?

Orders execute on Hyperliquid. Every fill leaves a public record you can check.
