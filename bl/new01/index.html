What can I trade?

Perpetuals on global stocks, indices, commodities and crypto, from one INR account.

Am I buying company shares?

No. You get exposure to a price, not ownership of shares, votes or dividends.

What is a perpetual contract?

A futures contract with no expiry date.

How do funding and liquidation work?

Funding is paid between longs and shorts at set intervals. If margin falls below the maintenance level, the position is closed. Both are shown before you trade.

Is BharatLiquid registered in India?

Registration details will be published before launch. [To confirm with legal.]

Who can join?

Indian residents aged 18 or over who complete KYC.
