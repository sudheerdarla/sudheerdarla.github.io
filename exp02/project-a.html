Project A: A staking app people trust with their savings

Flagship 0 to 1 case study. Longest of the four: it carries the full story from research to results.

Role
Lead product designer
Team
1 PM, 4 engineers
Timeline
6 months, 2022

Summary

  • Problem: people avoided staking because fees and lock-up terms were hidden until the last step.
  • What I did: owned research, structure, interface and handoff for the full deposit-to-withdrawal flow.
  • Result: $25M held within 6 months; fee-related support tickets down 35% (sample).

Context

Who the users were, what the product is, and why it mattered to the business. Two to four sentences.

Sample: Liquid staking lets people earn yield and keep a tradable token. The audience was crypto-curious savers who feared losing funds, so trust was the product.

The problem

State it as a question: How might we let a nervous first-time user stake with full knowledge of cost, time and risk?

  • Fees only appeared on the confirmation screen
  • Withdrawal time was buried in a help article
  • Support tickets showed confusion at three specific steps

Goals and constraints

Reduce confusion about feesFee-related support tickets

Constraints

  • Smart contract limits fixed the withdrawal delay
