Product decisions for an AI tool

Decision 1: Show the source for every generated line

Users cannot trust output they cannot check. Each brief line links to the part of the transcript it came from.

Decision 2: Make every output editable and undoable

Sample: the AI is wrong sometimes, so editing is a first-class action, not a fallback.

Decision 3: Say when the model is unsure

Sample: low-confidence lines are marked for review instead of presented as fact.
